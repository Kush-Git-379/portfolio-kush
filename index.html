About Me

Engineer by training,
builder by instinct.

I'm Kush Mehta, an electronics and communications engineering student at Nirma University with a minor in Data Science. My work sits at the intersection of hardware and software — designing circuits that fly, programming microcontrollers that sense, and writing code that makes sense of data.

As Electronics Lead for Team Arrow, Nirma's official aeromodelling club, I've led the electronics design and integration for competition aircraft, earning multiple podium finishes at SAE national and international events.

My specializations are Aerial Robotics and IoT — fields where precision engineering meets intelligent systems.
