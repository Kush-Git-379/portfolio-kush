Flagship Project Team Arrow 2024–2025

Autonomous VTOL Tricopter Tilt-Rotor Plane with Payload Delivery & Capture

Designed and built a hybrid fixed-wing/multirotor aircraft capable of vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) using a tricopter tilt-rotor configuration. The aircraft transitions between hover and forward flight modes autonomously using ArduPilot with custom parameter tuning. Key engineering challenges included designing the tilt-rotor servo mechanism, calibrating the flight controller for stable mode transitions, and integrating a payload delivery and capture system capable of precision drop at target coordinates. The electronics stack included custom power distribution, telemetry, GPS-assisted navigation, and a camera-based image processing pipeline for target detection.

Competition Journey SAE DDC · SAE Aero Design 2022–2026

Team Arrow — SAE Competition Electronics Lead

As Electronics Lead for Team Arrow, Nirma University's official aeromodelling club, I was responsible for the complete electronics design, integration, and debugging of competition aircraft across multiple SAE seasons. This included power systems design, ESC/motor selection, flight controller integration, telemetry setup, and sensor fusion. The role demanded rapid iteration under competition deadlines and cross-functional collaboration with mechanical and software sub-teams.
