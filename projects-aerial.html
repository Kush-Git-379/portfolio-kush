Autonomous Tilt-Rotor VTOL Tricopter with AGV Payload: SAE Aero Design East 2026

Electronics Head & Avionics Lead

Designed and built a hybrid tilt-rotor tricopter (Team Arrow Advanced-224) for SAE Aero Design East 2026. The aircraft combines a NACA 9325 lifting-body fuselage with S1223 rectangular wings and three T-Motor F80 Pro 2500KV motors, two forward-mounted tilting motors on a CFRP boom and one fixed tail motor. The system operates fully autonomously, carrying an Autonomous Ground Vehicle (AGV) payload that independently navigates a target zone using a magnetometer, ToF sensors, and IR line-following; qualifying for the competition's highest 14× capture score multiplier. A fully autonomous low-payload strategy was chosen after scoring analysis showed it yields ~52 points versus ~30 for a manual high-payload approach. As Electronics and Avionics Lead, I was responsible for the complete avionics stack: flight controller integration, GPS navigation, tilt mechanism actuation, power systems design, and Software-in-the-Loop simulation validation.

Engineering Highlights, Electronics & Avionics

  • Integrated TBS Lucid H7 flight controller with Here4 GPS for centimetre-level autonomous navigation
  • Built and validated full mission profile via Software-in-the-Loop (SITL) simulation before first real flight

  • Modal and harmonic analysis in ANSYS confirmed no resonance between motor vibration and wing natural frequencies
  • Full-body CFD in ANSYS Fluent with Spalart-Allmaras turbulence model; topology optimization via Fusion 360

Fixed-Wing Micro Class Aircraft with Water Payload: SAE Aero Design West 2025

Propulsion Lead

Designed and built a high-wing micro class aircraft (Team Arrow-325) for the SAE Aero Design West competition in Van Nuys, California, Team Arrow's first international SAE event. The aircraft carried a 67 oz water payload, completed a runway takeoff circuit, and landed within a defined zone. As Propulsion Lead, I engineered the entire power plant under a strict 400W electrical power limit; requiring careful motor and propeller matching to maximise thrust within the constraint. The aircraft placed 5th internationally in mission score.

Engineering Highlights, Propulsion

  • Selected Sunnysky X3520 560KV to deliver required 26 N thrust while staying within the 400W competition power ceiling at 70–80% throttle
  • Sized 16×10 inch propeller using Gabriel Staples equation; validated by practical thrust bench test against E-Calc predictions
  • Implemented flaps via symmetric aileron deflection, increasing CL by 20%+ to reduce takeoff distance
  • Teardrop fuselage selected over circular and square via three-way CFD comparison (0.705N drag vs 1.156N and 1.368N)
  • Topology optimization: landing gear 20% mass reduction, wing ribs 15% mass reduction via Fusion 360
  • Water sloshing CG analysis confirmed stable static margin across −10° to +15° angle of attack
  • CFRP main spar introduced for first time; Altair HyperMesh used for wing meshing

High Payload-Fraction Fixed-Wing MCA: SAE DDC 2025

Propulsion Head & Pilot

Iterating on the 2024 entry, the team scaled up to a larger box-type fuselage monoplane targeting a 2.5 kg payload with an improved payload fraction of 0.670. As Propulsion Head and one of two competition pilots, I led the full power plant redesign, upgrading to a larger AT2820 T-Motor 1250KV with a 12×6 propeller and 100A ESC to handle the increased thrust requirement of 28 N. The aircraft introduced transient CFD stall simulation with a rotating domain and improved topology optimization, achieving 30% mass reduction per rib.

High Payload-Fraction Fixed-Wing MCA; SAE DDC 2024

Propulsion Engineer

First major competition entry with Team Arrow. The aircraft achieved 2nd place overall in the SAE DDC 2024 Micro category with a payload fraction of 0.635, the primary competition metric. As Propulsion Engineer, I was responsible for motor selection, propeller sizing, battery and ESC selection, servo torque calculations, and thrust bench testing. The design philosophy centred on minimising empty weight (772 g) to maximise payload fraction, a constraint that shaped every component decision.
